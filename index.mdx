B3D Nav ports the modal object manipulation workflow from Blender directly into Unreal Engine 5's editor. If you already know Blender's `G`, `R`, and `S` shortcuts, you can start transforming actors in UE5 the same way — no configuration required.

## What B3D Nav Does

B3D Nav intercepts editor input through a custom `IInputProcessor` and routes it through a modal state machine. When modal mode is active, pressing `G`, `R`, or `S` enters a transform — then mouse movement or numeric key entry drives the transformation until you confirm with **Left Click** or **Enter**, or cancel with **Right Click** or **Escape**.

## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the plugin">
    Copy the `BlenderControls` plugin folder into your project's `Plugins/` directory, then enable it via **Edit → Plugins**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate modal mode">
    Click the **Blender Controls** toggle button in the Level Editor toolbar to activate modal mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select actors and transform">
    Select one or more actors in the viewport. Press `G` to grab, `R` to rotate, or `S` to scale. Move your mouse to apply the transform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm or cancel">
    **Left Click** or **Enter** confirms the transform. **Right Click** or **Escape** cancels it and leaves the actors unchanged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A more fully-featured fork of this project is available at [Blend4Real](https://github.com/Nehon/Blend4Real) if you need additional Blender workflow features beyond what B3D Nav provides.
</Note>
